Chapter 01 — 2026 Regulatory, Tax & Market Baselines

Chapter 1: Twelve Core Operating Criteria Based on Vietnam’s Latest 2026 Regulations, Customs Inspection Requirements, and Market Conditions

Based on Vietnam’s latest enacted measures between July 2025 and October 2026—including the Enterprise Law Amendment, the 2024 Social Insurance Law, the 2024 Fire Prevention and Rescue Law, the 2025 Personal Income Tax Law, the Ministry of Industry and Trade’s new textile inspection regime, as well as China-Korea cross-border customs practices and e-commerce platform fee rates—this project establishes twelve key regulatory and commercial benchmarks underpinning contract drafting and the financial model.

Benchmark 01 ｜ Corporate Governance and Voting Thresholds Article 59, Luật Doanh nghiệp 2020

65% Statutory Minimum Voting Threshold for Limited Liability Companies and UBO Look-Through Disclosure

Vietnam’s 2020 Enterprise Law (Số 59/2020/QH14), Article 59(3) and Article 59(5), expressly provides that resolutions of the members’ council (Hội đồng thành viên) of a limited liability company must, “unless the company charter provides otherwise at a higher percentage,” secure the consent of members representing at least 65% of contributed capital for ordinary resolutions and written voting (Lấy ý kiến bằng văn bản); special resolutions to amend the charter, restructure or dissolve the company, or dispose of more than 50% of total assets require at least 75%. The HCMC Business Registration Office does not permit the charter to reduce the threshold to 51%. Accordingly, the controlling investor (Lead Financial Partner) is locked in at 67% (minimum ≥65%), while the Taiwan side (Partner B) is capped at up to 33%. In addition, under the 2025 Enterprise Law Amendment (Luật số 76/2025/QH15) and Thông tư 121/2026/TT-BTC, effective July 2025, every individual holding ≥25% must transparently disclose beneficial ownership (UBO), preventing disputes involving undisclosed nominee holdings.

Baseline 02 ｜ Foreign-Invested Retail Licensing and Rent-Free Period Decree 09/2018/NĐ-CP, Article 20

MOIT approval timeline in Hanoi and zero pre-opening rent-burn mechanism through a "combined dual-license application"

Pursuant to Articles 20, 22 and 25 of Decree 09/2018/NĐ-CP, although a foreign investor's first physical retail outlet is exempt from the economic needs test (ENT), following initial review by the HCMC Department of Home Industry and Commerce (DOIT), the application must be submitted to the Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) in Hanoi for ministerial approval. The end-to-end process, from securing an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) to DICA account capital verification and obtaining both retail licenses, typically takes 3.5–4.5 months. To avoid incurring monthly rent of 70,000,000 VND while licensing is pending, this project adopts a "conditional Letter of Intent (MOU) first" approach: the formal lease and its 45-day rent-free fit-out period commence only after the ERC has been issued and DICA capital verification has been completed. In accordance with Article 20, the foreign-invested retail operating license and the establishment permit for the first physical outlet are submitted together as a single consolidated dossier, directly saving approximately $9,000 USD in occupancy costs during the approval period.

Baseline 03 ｜ Mandatory Testing of Imported Textiles Thông tư 33/2026/TT-BCT

Formaldehyde and Azo Dye Testing under QCVN 01:2017/BCT (2026 Revision) and the CR Mark

Pursuant to Thông tư 21/2017/TT-BCT and Thông tư 33/2026/TT-BCT and Thông tư 37/2026/TT-BCT (Amending 01:2026 QCVN 01:2017/BCT), effective 1 July 2026, all ready-to-wear garments under Chapters 61 and 62 that are officially imported for sale, comprising Category 2 textiles intended for direct skin contact (formaldehyde content ≤ 75 mg/kg and decomposable aromatic amines from azo dyes ≤ 30 mg/kg), must undergo testing under "Method 7 (batch sampling inspection)" through an MOIT-designated laboratory, such as Quatest 3 in HCMC or TQC CGLOBAL, which must issue a certificate of conformity. Products may be placed on sale only after the CR conformity mark (Dấu hợp quy CR) and the Vietnamese supplementary label (Tem phụ) have been affixed to the hangtag (OZ Việt Nam 2026 Guide). The project plans to consolidate shipments into containers on a biweekly basis, with each shipment comprising 2,500–3,000 units. Testing costs are approximately $120–$150 USD per shipment, equivalent to only $0.06 USD per unit.

Baseline 04 ｜ China–Korea Cross-Border Rules of Origin ACFTA Form E & VKFTA

20% Duty Provision on Spot-Bought Korean Dongdaemun Goods and Four Conditions for Form E Third-Party Invoicing from China

(1) 20% sourcing from Korea's Dongdaemun district: Under the Vietnam–Korea Free Trade Agreement (VKFTA), garments must comply with the "Yarn Forward" rule, with the Korean manufacturing plant issuing an origin statement for submission to Korean Customs or the Korea Chamber of Commerce and Industry (KCCI) to obtain Form VK. Seoul's Dongdaemun district (apM Luxe / U:US) consists of cash-based wholesale stalls and cannot provide factory-level yarn traceability documentation. Formal customs declarations therefore require payment of the 20% MFN import duty, which is provided for conservatively in this model, with a weighted allocation across the entire store of +$0.32 USD per unit.
(2) 80% sourcing from Guangzhou/Hangzhou, China: Procurement must be restricted to supply-chain enterprises with export-filing qualifications or 1688 factories capable of obtaining a valid C/O Form E and securing 0% duty. Where third-party invoicing is used, the transaction must satisfy the four Vietnamese Customs requirements for Form E third-party invoicing: Field 1 must identify the actual Chinese producer; Field 7 must identify the third-party invoicing company; the invoice number in Field 10 must be consistent; and Field 13 must be marked Third Party Invoicing.

Baseline 05 ｜ 2025–2026 Administrative Restructuring Nghị quyết 1685/NQ-UBTVQH15

Abolition of District-Level Administration in HCMC's Former District 3 and Legal Address Requirements Following Consolidation into Three Directly Administered Wards

Pursuant to Resolution 1685/NQ-UBTVQH15 of the National Assembly Standing Committee, effective 1 July 2025, HCMC has abolished the administrative tier corresponding to urban districts (Quận), with all 14 wards of the former District 3 consolidated into three new directly administered wards:
• Nhiêu Lộc Ward (Phường Nhiêu Lộc): Comprises the entirety of the former Wards 9, 11, 12 and 14, including the core womenswear retail corridor along Trần Quang Diệu and Lê Văn Sỹ Streets.
• Bàn Cờ Ward (Phường Bàn Cờ): Comprises the entirety of the former Wards 1, 2, 3 and 5, together with part of the former Ward 4.
• Xuân Hòa Ward (Phường Xuân Hòa): Comprises the former Võ Thị Sáu Ward (Phường Võ Thị Sáu) and the remainder of the former Ward 4.
All leases, business registrations (ERCs), electronic invoices and Vietnamese supplementary product labels must use the new legal addresses, such as Phường Nhiêu Lộc, TP. Hồ Chí Minh.

Baseline 06 ｜ Fire Safety Requirements for Combined Townhouse Store and Warehouse Luật PCCC 2024, Article 21

Technical Requirements for Vertical EI60 Fire and Smoke Doors and Façade Egress Windows under the 2024 Law on Fire Prevention and Rescue

Under Article 21 of the 2024 Law on Fire Prevention, Fighting, and Rescue (Luật số 55/2024/QH15), effective July 2025, tube-style shophouses combining commercial retail with flammable goods storage (4F storing over 10,000 apparel units and packaging cartons) must install independent fire compartmentalization (adding EI60-rated self-closing fire and smoke-rated doors between the 4F warehouse and interior stairwell to prevent the chimney effect), explosion-proof conduit wiring throughout the building, strictly prohibit exterior signage from blocking 2F–4F balcony emergency exits, and strictly prohibit employee sleeping quarters inside the building. All of the above works are fully included in the project's 485M VND ($19,094 USD) baseline renovation budget.

Benchmark 07 | 2026 Individual Landlord Rental Tax Regime Luật Thuế TNCN 2025 Art. 7

Tax-Exempt Threshold Raised to 500M VND/Year, Business License Tax Abolished, and PIT Levied Only on Excess

Under the Amended VAT Law, Article 7 of the 2025 Personal Income Tax Law, and Nghị quyết 198/2025/QH15, effective January 1, 2026: (1) The tax-exempt threshold for individual property leasing is raised significantly from the old 100M VND/year to 500M VND/year, and the Business License Fee (Lệ phí môn bài) is completely abolished; (2) 5% Personal Income Tax (PIT) is now levied solely on the portion of annual rent exceeding 500M VND, i.e., $\text{Annual PIT} = (\text{Annual Rent} - 500\text{M}) \times 5\%$, while 5% VAT applies to the full rental amount. Based on net monthly rent of 70M VND (840M VND/year), full-year VAT is 42M VND and PIT is only 17M VND, bringing total annual rental tax to 59M VND (4.917M VND/month / $194 USD, effective tax rate 7.02%), saving 25M VND annually compared to the prior regime. Furthermore, per Nghị định 320/2025/NĐ-CP, single transactions exceeding 5M VND must be paid via corporate bank transfer with a signed procurement schedule (Bảng kê) to qualify as deductible Corporate Income Tax (CIT) expenses.

Benchmark 08 | 2026 Labor, Social Insurance & PIT Regime Luật BHXH 2024 & NĐ 253/2026

Mandatory SI for Part-Timers, 1.2M VND Tax-Free Meal Allowance Cap, and 15.5M VND PIT Deduction

(1) Per Article 2(1)(l) of the 2024 Social Insurance Law, part-time employees on contracts of 1 month or longer earning at least the minimum SI base (reference wage 2,530,000 VND) are subject to mandatory social insurance contributions; and per Article 31, all fixed monthly allowances stated in the labor contract are included in the SI base. (2) Per Nghị định 253/2026/NĐ-CP effective July 1, 2026, the PIT-exempt cap for cash meal allowances is officially raised to 1,200,000 VND / person / month. (3) Per Nghị quyết 110/2025/UBTVQH15, starting in 2026 the personal PIT family deduction is raised to 15.5M VND / month (6.2M VND/month per dependent).

Benchmark 09 | 2026 E-Commerce Platform Fees & Return Rules Shopee & TikTok Shop 2026

21%–25% Marketplace Take Rates & 'No Commission Refund on Post-Delivery Returns' Channel Mix Strategy

Shopee (57.5% market share) and TikTok Shop (39.6% market share) control 97.1% of Vietnam's e-commerce traffic, yet May–August 2026 fee hikes on both platforms raised women's apparel fixed commissions to 13.5%–16.5% and transaction processing fees to 6.0% (charged even on buyer shipping fees), plus a 3,000 VND per-order handling fee and campaign fees, pushing total take rates to 21.0%–25.2%. Moreover, effective April 1, 2026, TikTok Shop does not refund the 14%–15% commission or 6% transaction fee on post-delivery returns. Accordingly, this project establishes a golden channel mix of "50% Nhieu Loc Ward Flagship Store (1.55% friction fee) + 30% FB/IG/Zalo DM & Official Website Direct (5.0% friction fee) + 20% Shopee/TikTok Marketplace Traffic (~24.5% friction fee)", keeping the storewide weighted channel friction fee tightly controlled at 7.29% ($0.94 USD/unit).

Benchmark 10 | Fast-Fashion Broken-Size & Seasonal Markdown Model Realized Net ASP Modeling

Realized Net ASP Incorporating 6.8% Broken-Size Promo & End-of-Season Clearance Dilution

In fast-fashion women's apparel operations carrying 300+ active SKUs, 100% of inventory cannot sell out at full tag price. Based on conservative industry benchmarks, this report models: 76% of SKUs sold at full price, 15% broken-size items (Lẻ size) discounted by 30% (4.5% markdown loss), 8% end-of-season tail stock cleared at 50% off (4.0% gross loss, split 50/50 with the Taiwan side under JV supply terms, leaving 2.0% borne by Vietnam), and 0.3% fitting/PR shrinkage. After deducting the 6.8% blended markdown dilution, storewide Realized Net ASP (ex-VAT) is adjusted from the ex-VAT tag price of $13.85 USD to $12.91 USD (327,914 VND), ensuring high reliability for break-even and payback projections.

Benchmark 11 | Supply Chain Exclusivity & Operational Continuity Exclusivity & Step-in Rights

Vietnam-Wide Exclusive Supply, Anti-Shadow-Store Non-Compete & Marketing/Procurement Step-In Mechanism

To safeguard the JV's market exclusivity and operational continuity, the Joint Venture Agreement (JVA) stipulates: (1) Vietnam-Wide Exclusivity & Non-Compete: Prohibits partners from privately wholesaling identical apparel styles to HCMC's An Dong Market (Chợ An Đông) or operating competing cross-border shadow stores on Shopee/TikTok; (2) Operational Continuity Step-In Rights: If the partner fails to deliver a qualified new-arrival selection list for 14 consecutive days, drops below the ad performance stop-loss (ROAS < 2.8x), or halts creative updates, the Vietnam JV has the right to immediately activate a local backup team to take over ad buying and direct China/Korea sourcing; (3) Default Equity Discounted Call Option: Establishes an explicit NAV-discounted buyout formula for material breach or shareholder deadlock.

Benchmark 12 | Local Petite Sizing & Low-Cost Customer Acquisition Petite Sizing & O2O Flywheel

HCMC Petite Fit Standards (153–158cm) & Three Zero-Commission Traffic Flywheels

Native Taiwanese women's apparel patterns are designed for an average height of 160–162cm, causing dragging hems and oversized waists when imported directly to Vietnam. This project establishes strict Vietnamese Petite Sizing selection standards (avg. height 153–158cm, weight 42–50kg): ≥85% S/M size mix, 38–44cm cropped tops, anti-exposure skorts (essential for motorbike commuters), and 94–97cm trouser lengths, paired with an in-store "Free 15-Minute Hemming Service", "HCMC University KOC Gifting/Seeded Shoots (monthly cost <$195 USD)", and "Zalo OA Wednesday New-Arrival Private Push" to build a high-conversion local acquisition flywheel.
